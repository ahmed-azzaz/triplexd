Usage

  • Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter “T” on all sides.
  • Minimum width: about 120px for the full lockup; below that, use the mark on its own.
  • Gold is reserved for the decision element (the node and the full stop) — never recolour the wordmark gold.
  • Use the reversed version on petrol or any dark photo; use monochrome where colour printing isn’t available.
  • Don’t stretch, rotate, add shadows, or change the letter-spacing of the wordmark.
  • Don’t place the light logo on a busy or light-clashing background — switch to reversed.
